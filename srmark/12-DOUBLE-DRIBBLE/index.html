About the Brand

About the Brand

"We bring sustainability to sports to infuse into your lifestyle." Double Dribble stems from a passion for sports and a commitment to sustainability, transforming basketballs, volleyballs, baseballs, and other retired sports equipment into accessories, lifestyle goods, and handicraft. Through design, upcycling, and creativity, we provide a brand new sports waste upcycling system that allows sport related goods at the end of their life cycle to check back in the game.
