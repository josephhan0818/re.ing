About the Brand

Green Footprint Int'l Co., Ltd. was founded in 2021 as a social enterprise established by the Taiwan Toy Library Association, building on the association's more than 20 years of experience in toy recycling and parent-child education. We have observed that up to 90% of children's toys in Taiwan are made of plastic, yet their average lifespan is only around six months. To extend the lifespan of toys and support a low-carbon transition, we established Toy Box in Taoyuan in 2023 — Taiwan's first specialised second-hand toy store. Since 2024, Green Footprint has officially entered a new stage in the circular economy, transforming discarded toys into recycled plastic pellets and 3D printing filaments. We believe that only by moving the concept of "recycling" from the end of the process to the very beginning can we truly realise resource circularity and create a more sustainable path for the next generation.

Design Works

re-ing Sustainable Balance Board

The re-ing Sustainable Balance Board is a balance toy inspired by the shape of a potato chip, merging environmental responsibility with aesthetic design. Made from over 50% recycled plastic sourced from the Taiwan Toy Library, the product is crafted entirely from HDPE to ensure 100% recyclability. Utilizing mass-production techniques from the furniture industry, it breathes new life into post-consumer plastic and exemplifies circular economy in action. The design addresses the challenge of integrating recycled materials into the toy industry. Through balance-based play, the board encourages children to develop an early understanding of sustainable resource values. Its height and curvature are precisely calculated for stability and safety, making it suitable for users of all ages. It functions both as a balance board and as a seating cushion, with a hollow structure that reduces weight while increasing usability. The board's rounded form and vibrant marbled colors—created naturally by variations in the recycled material—make it not only a functional toy, but also a playful design accent for the home. Made from durable, waterproof, and UV-resistant materials, it is ideal for use in a variety of settings including balconies, gardens, and living rooms. Compared to traditional plastic toys, the re-ing Sustainable Balance Board combines material innovation, family interaction, and design-led education. It offers a hands-on, accessible way to experience circular design, setting a new standard for the sustainable toy industry by making environmental awareness tangible, fun, and integrated into everyday life.
