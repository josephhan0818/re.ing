About the Brand

Since 1959, Simbalion has been leaving a vibrant mark on Taiwan. From childhood crayons to our iconic, generation-spanning permanent markers, we consistently break the mold. In 2020, we established the "Imagination Factory," an interactive art museum, to drive cross-generational creative experimentation and aesthetic exploration. We invite you to join us and ignite your imagination!
