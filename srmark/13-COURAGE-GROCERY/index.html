About the Brand

Courage Grocery sells the ordinary things of everyday life — rice, oil, salt, soy sauce, vinegar, and tea. But every single item has been touched by the magic of courage, and carries extraordinary depth and richness. Running the store has never been fearless; every step is taken with real, trembling fear. And because of that, the landscape that unfolds when you gather the courage to move forward is seen with great clarity. Every product on the shelf is made with someone's courage, and each comes with a quiet note written by someone, somewhere — because as Reverend Billy Graham said: "Courage is contagious. When one person takes courage, the spines of others are stiffened."
