01

Project Positioning
& Design Challenge

RE:TWIST Circular Spin Project transforms marine waste and recycled toys into lucky figurines, co-created by designers from Taiwan, the Netherlands and Spain. Moving beyond form-driven design, this edition develops each figure as a character IP with its own background story, combining visual identity, emotional connection and production feasibility.

02

Material Regeneration
& Environmental Value

The project does not merely recycle materials; it redefines their role. Through design, marine waste and discarded toys re-enter the product lifecycle as sustainable characters with stories and personalities, making circular design visible, collectable and easier for the public to understand.

03

Cultural Translation
& International Co-creation

Luck is a shared language across cultures. Drawing from cultural memory, local symbols and everyday experience, each designer reinterprets ideas of blessing, protection and good fortune. Each character is not only a figurine, but also an IP carrying cultural perspective and sustainable storytelling.

04

Exhibition Experience
& Future Potential

By using capsule toys and collectible figurines as familiar entry points, the project makes sustainability more approachable. With character settings and background stories, the exhibition presents not only products, but also emotional entry points between audiences and characters. Its future potential extends to material education, touring exhibitions and circular product development.
