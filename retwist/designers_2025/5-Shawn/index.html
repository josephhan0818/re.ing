Mute Cat: Silence is Golden

L 3.3cm × W 2.9cm × H 4.3cm
SHAWN
Automotive Design Language
Silent Resistance
Environmental Consciousness

Design Story

Mute Cat: Silence is Golden is a Lucky Cat that neither speaks, waves, nor follows. It has no tail, no face, no beckoning paw. Rendered with the sculptural language of automotive design, its simplified geometric form holds both tension and calm.

Flip it over, and you'll find a stamp engraved with: "Do Not Tail (Do Not Tell) / Silence is Golden." This is not a slogan, but a quiet protest. In an age overwhelmed by noise, opinions, and blurred boundaries, the cat asserts itself through stillness. No tail—no following. No mouth—no telling. Silence—a conscious stance of resistance and preservation.

Crafted from recycled marine polystyrene, materials once polluting our oceans are now reimagined as a compact art object with environmental and philosophical depth. The mold-split production approach speaks to both sustainability and manufacturability, while also metaphorically expressing the tension between separation and integration—of self, society, and nature. Mute Cat is not a mascot—it's a minimalist fable. In a world too loud to think clearly, silence isn't apathy—it's clarity. And sometimes, the strongest presence is the one that simply refuses to perform.

About SHAWN

SHAWN

Based in Taiwan

Shawn Lee, Chairman of the Industrial Design Development Association of Taipei, is a specialist in Transportation Design with a strong background in industrial design. He began his career at Taiwan's automotive brand Luxgen, focusing on both interior and exterior styling. After transitioning from corporate life, Shawn founded In Motion Design Co., Ltd., expanding his practice into multimedia and animation to explore new intersections between design and visual storytelling.

He established In Motion Space—a platform that embodies the philosophy "Everything flows; nothing stands still." Through this space, he encourages continuous creativity and artistic expression, especially among the new generation of creators. Since 2015, he has taught Transportation Design at Huafan University, advocating for a mindset rooted in action and purpose—"not creating for the sake of creating," but designing with clear direction and critical thinking. Deeply aware of the importance of international collaboration, Shawn actively promotes Taiwanese creative brands on the global stage through platforms such as "Flash Point," realizing the In Motion spirit—keeping art and design in continuous movement while advancing Taiwan's creative momentum worldwide.
