castaway cat Figure

L 3.1cm × W 2.8cm × H 4.2cm
KAIRI EGUCHI
Ecological Cycle
Natural Inspiration
Life Renewal

Design Story

Taking a standard Japanese maneki-neko (beckoning cat) as the motif, I modeled the form as if its details had melted away. The design evokes the feeling of a piece of glass that has drifted ashore, its sharp corners smoothed by the sea to become sea glass. Because the maneki-neko is such a famous symbol, people can still recognize it even with minimal detail. This new form also allows it to harmonize beautifully with modern interior spaces.

About KAIRI EGUCHI

Designer and Creative Director. Since 2008, based in Osaka and Tokyo, he has been dedicated to the belief of "building meaningful relationships between people and objects through design." He co-creates design and identity with startups, local factories, and traditional artisans.
